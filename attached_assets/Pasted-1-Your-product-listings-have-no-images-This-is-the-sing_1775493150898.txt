1. Your product listings have no images
This is the single biggest conversion killer on your homepage.

Your “Featured Hardware” section shows multiple products with:

“No Image”  
AWDP‑EE‑LOLE
AWDP‑EE‑BLIO
AWDP‑EE‑IITL
AWDP‑EE‑OBLO
