High-level assessment
•	Positioning: Strong niche (replacement window/door hardware, obsolete parts, veteran-owned) and clear value props.
•	Big gaps: Technical performance (likely), structured data, internal linking depth, and conversion UX around “Free Parts ID” and shopping paths.
•	Priority: Treat this as an e commerce + lead-gen hybrid: optimize for both product sales and photo-upload leads.
1. Technical & performance
1.1 Core Web Vitals & speed
•	Likely issues:
o	Images: Product images and hero graphics may not be fully optimized (no WebP/AVIF, oversized dimensions, missing lazy-loading).
o	JS/CSS bloat: Theme + plugins can add render-blocking JS/CSS and slow TTFB/LCP.
•	Actions:
o	Image optimization:
	Convert large images to WebP; set proper width/height; enable lazy-loading for below-the-fold images.
o	CSS/JS:
	Defer non-critical JS, inline critical CSS, and remove unused CSS/JS where possible.
o	Caching/CDN:
	Enable full-page caching and use a CDN for static assets.
o	Measure:
	Run PageSpeed Insights on: /, a category page, a product page, and the Free Parts ID page. Fix LCP/CLS/INP issues first.
1.2 Mobile & responsiveness
•	Check:
o	Tap targets (buttons/links) are large enough and spaced.
o	Forms (photo upload, contact) are easy to use on mobile.
•	Actions:
o	Ensure sticky CTA on mobile (e.g., “Upload Photos for Free ID” or “Shop Parts”) so users always have a next step.
1.3 Security & basics
•	HTTPS: Ensure all resources load over HTTPS (no mixed content).
•	404/500 handling:
o	Custom 404 page with search + links to “Shop Parts” and “Free Parts ID”.
•	XML sitemap & robots.txt:
o	Confirm sitemap is auto-generated and referenced in robots.txt.
o	Block only true junk (admin, cart fragments, etc.), not categories/products.
2. On-page SEO & structure
2.1 Titles & meta descriptions
•	Homepage:
o	Current title is strong but can be slightly more keyword-targeted:
	Example:
	Replacement Window & Door Hardware | Obsolete Parts Experts | All Window Door Parts
o	Meta description should emphasize: 35,000+ parts, obsolete hardware, Free Parts ID, veteran-owned, fast shipping.
•	Category pages:
o	Each category should target a primary keyword:
	“Casement Window Operators”, “Patio Door Rollers”, “Window Sash Balances”, etc.
o	Write unique meta descriptions with problem + solution language.
2.2 Headings & content hierarchy
•	Homepage:
o	Ensure a single, strong H1 (e.g., “Replacement Window & Door Hardware – Obsolete Parts Experts”).
o	Use H2s for sections: “Shop by Problem”, “Featured Hardware”, “Browse by Part Type”, “Why Choose All Window Door Parts?”, “Free Parts ID”.
•	Product pages:
o	H1 = product name with key terms (e.g., “30in Window Channel Balance – AWDP-TE-RLP-RB”).
o	Add short intro paragraph: what it fits, common window types/brands, symptoms it fixes.
2.3 Internal linking
•	From homepage:
o	Link “Shop 35,000+ Parts” to a master catalog or “Shop All Parts” page.
o	Each “Shop by Problem” tile should link to a problem-focused landing page (not just a generic category) that explains:
	Symptoms → likely parts → featured products → CTA to Free Parts ID if unsure.
•	From blog/FAQ (if present):
o	Use how-to content to link into specific categories and products.
3. Content, messaging & trust
3.1 Problem-solution framing (already strong—double down)
•	“Shop by Problem” is gold. Expand it into full funnels:
o	Each problem page should include:
	Plain-language explanation (“If your window won’t stay up, the sash balance is likely worn out.”)
	Photo examples of the issue.
	Top 3–6 parts that usually fix it.
	CTA: “Still not sure? Upload photos for Free Parts ID.”
3.2 Free Parts ID as primary lead magnet
•	This is your unique superpower—treat it like the main product.
•	Actions:
o	Dedicated landing page /free-parts-id/ with:
	Step-by-step instructions (how to take photos, what angles, include tape measure, etc.).
	Example before/after: “Customer sent this photo → we identified this exact part → shipped in 3 days.”
	Short form with photo upload, name, email, phone, and “How urgent is this?”
o	Add prominent CTAs sitewide: header, footer, and in product/category pages (“Can’t confirm this part? Upload photos for Free ID.”).
3.3 Social proof & authority
•	You already have strong testimonials—make them work harder:
o	Add star ratings and review counts on product pages (if possible).
o	Create a “Trusted by” section: homeowners, contractors, property managers, HOAs, etc.
o	Add about page depth: founder story, 40+ years timeline, veteran background, photos of the team/warehouse.
4. UX & conversion optimization
4.1 Navigation & IA
•	Top nav should clearly separate:
o	Shop Parts (with dropdown: Casement Operators, Sash Balances, Patio Door Rollers, Weatherstripping, Locks, Handles, Hinges, etc.)
o	Shop by Problem
o	Free Parts ID
o	About / Contact
•	Add a prominent search bar (people often know brand/model numbers or rough part names).
4.2 Product page UX
•	Must-haves:
o	Clear price, availability (“In Stock”)
o	Compatibility info: brands, series, years, and common window/door types.
o	Measurement diagrams with labeled dimensions.
o	“Not sure this fits?” → link to Free Parts ID.
4.3 Checkout & friction
•	Minimize steps to checkout; avoid forced account creation if possible.
•	Show shipping options and estimates early (cart/mini-cart).
•	Reassure with security badges, “Secure Shopping Guarantee”, and clear return policy.
5. Schema, local & brand signals
5.1 Structured data
•	Implement JSON-LD for:
o	Organization (name, logo, phone, sameAs social profiles).
o	Product (name, SKU, price, availability, brand, image, description).
o	BreadcrumbList for categories/products.
o	FAQPage on Free Parts ID and key problem pages.
5.2 Local & trust signals
•	Even if you ship nationwide, emphasize:
o	Physical location (city/state), phone number, business hours.
o	“Veteran Owned Business” badge and any relevant certifications.
•	Create/optimize Google Business Profile with photos, Q&A, and reviews.
6. Analytics, tracking & growth
6.1 Measurement
•	Ensure you have:
o	GA4 (or equivalent) with e commerce tracking.
o	Event tracking for:
	Photo uploads (Free Parts ID).
	Add to cart, checkout start, purchase.
•	Set up funnels:
o	“Shop by Problem → Product → Add to Cart → Purchase”
o	“Free Parts ID → Form submit → Quote/Order”
6.2 Content & SEO growth
•	Build a knowledge base / blog around:
o	“How to identify your window sash balance”
o	“How to measure a patio door roller”
o	“Signs your weatherstripping needs replacement”
•	Each article should:
o	Include step-by-step photos.
o	Link to relevant categories/products.
o	End with “Still unsure? Upload photos for Free Parts ID.”
